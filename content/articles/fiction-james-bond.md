He wears a perfectly fitted suit, drives fast cars, uses clever gadgets and always introduces himself the same way: Bond, James Bond. For more than seventy years, the British secret agent known as 007 has been one of the most famous characters in fiction. He has appeared in novels, films, video games and comics, and his name has become a symbol of style, danger and adventure.

## The Creator

James Bond was created by Ian Fleming, a British journalist and writer. During the Second World War, Fleming worked for British naval intelligence. He helped plan secret operations and met many real spies and officers. These experiences gave him ideas for his stories.

In 1953, Fleming published his first Bond novel, Casino Royale, which he wrote at his holiday home in Jamaica. He went on to write twelve novels and two collections of short stories about the agent. Fleming said he chose the name James Bond because he wanted something simple and plain, and he borrowed it from the author of a book about birds.

## Who Is 007?

In the stories, James Bond works for MI6, Britain's foreign intelligence service. His code number is 007. The double zero means he has a licence to kill, so he is allowed to use deadly force on his missions. His boss is known only by the letter M, and the clever scientist Q supplies him with gadgets.

Bond is brave, intelligent and calm under pressure. He is an expert in fighting, driving, shooting and gambling. In the films, he enjoys fine food and drinks and is famous for ordering his martini shaken, not stirred. His missions usually take him to beautiful locations around the world, where he must stop a powerful villain with a dangerous plan, often to take over or destroy the world.

## Bond on the Big Screen

The character became truly world-famous through films. The first Bond film, Dr. No, was released in 1962, with Sean Connery as Bond. Its success led to one of the longest-running film series in history, with more than twenty-five official films.

Several actors have played the role. Sean Connery, George Lazenby, Roger Moore, Timothy Dalton, Pierce Brosnan and Daniel Craig each gave Bond a different personality. Roger Moore's Bond was lighter and full of jokes, while Daniel Craig's Bond, starting with Casino Royale in 2006, was tougher and more emotional. Every few years, fans around the world eagerly discuss who will be the next actor to play 007.

The films are famous for their opening sequences, exciting chase scenes and theme songs sung by popular artists. Gadgets are a big part of the fun, from cars with hidden weapons and ejector seats to watches with lasers. The Aston Martin DB5, which first appeared in Goldfinger in 1964, became one of the most famous cars in film history. Bond's villains are also memorable, such as Goldfinger, Blofeld and Jaws, a giant with metal teeth.

## A Changing Hero

The early Bond stories reflect the attitudes of the 1950s and 1960s, and some of them now seem old-fashioned. The way the early films showed women, in particular, is often criticised today. Over time, the series has changed. Women have taken on stronger roles, such as Judi Dench playing M, the head of MI6. Recent films show Bond as a more complex character with feelings, doubts and painful memories.

## Conclusion

James Bond began as a character in a spy novel written by a former intelligence officer. He grew into a worldwide icon of action and adventure. By changing with the times while keeping his famous style, Agent 007 has stayed exciting for audiences across many generations.
